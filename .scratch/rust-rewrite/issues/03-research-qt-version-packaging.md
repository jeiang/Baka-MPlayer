# Research: Qt 6 vs Qt 5 and cross-platform packaging
Type: research
Status: open
Blocked by:

## Question

Qt 5 is end of life. What does a Qt 6 Widgets app need on Windows, Linux, and macOS to ship (windeployqt, macdeployqt, AppImage or Flatpak), including libmpv bundling, code signing and notarization, and LGPL linking obligations (dynamic vs static) given the repo license in `LICENSE`? What from Qt5 used here (`QtWinExtras`, `x11extras`) has no Qt 6 equivalent?
